About

Researching better ways to schedule and use computing resources.

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Kansas with research interests in High-Performance Computing and Machine Learning. My research focuses on scheduling and resource allocation for computationally intensive workloads, particularly online scheduling of moldable task graphs.

I am interested in designing efficient scheduling strategies that improve resource utilization and reduce application completion time. My work includes processor and multi-resource allocation, scheduling algorithms, performance modeling, task-priority policies, Bayesian optimization, workflow scheduling, and empirical evaluation of HPC systems.

Research

High-Performance Computing & Intelligent Scheduling

Online Scheduling

Scheduling moldable task graphs when tasks become available dynamically as dependencies complete.

Resource Allocation

Designing and evaluating processor-allocation and multi-resource strategies for efficient execution.

Optimization

Using Bayesian optimization and data-driven methods to tune scheduling parameters with fewer expensive evaluations.

Current direction: Multi-resource online scheduling involving processors, memory, cache, and bandwidth, with intelligent optimization for adaptive resource management.
